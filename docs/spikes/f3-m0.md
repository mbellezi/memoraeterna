## 4. Step 5: B4, candidates per note, `d` and the reverse guard

Batch = every note exists and judges its fused top 20 (D19's reference, F2's barrier). Arrival = roots released one
at a time behind the re-match fence (3.17): a note's text, vector and graph lists see released roots only (its own
root included), then the guard adds every earlier, decided note x among its vector top 2k = 40 with
`cos(x, note) ≥ x.kth_cosine` (x's 20th vector candidate when x was decided). Both directions of a pair are scored at
its first encounter, so `d` = candidate entries whose pair was already scored. Per 200 notes; calls at
`rankPairsPerCall` 32 (MLX) and 16 (GGUF). The simulation's batch lists equal the export's lists (the product's queries
and fusion function), in the same order, on every note of every profile.

- **Why arrival costs more.** A new note's own 20 candidates are earlier notes that never saw it, so almost every
  pair is new; only pairs inside one release step (a root's own notes) are shared. The guard is lossless for the
  vector signal but over-inclusive: an early note decided among few released notes keeps a low k-th cosine (or none,
  with fewer than 20 candidates), so many later notes complete it.
- **Agreement with batch (D19's 200-order replay is T51's; this is the S0 or manifest order only).** Judged-pair Jaccard
  0.40 (ENV-F), 0.62 (ENV-G), 0.71 and 0.78 (ENV-C draws). Batch pairs that arrival never judges: 112 of 1,771 on ENV-F
  and 71 of 2,197 on ENV-G (59 cross-root), 7 and 0 on ENV-C. They are surfaced from the earlier note's side by text or
  graph, the signals D19 says are not refreshed in reverse. Tuning recall is unaffected (26/26).
- **A refreshed k-th cosine (diagnostic, not a design).** Using x's k-th cosine over all released notes at the time
  the later note arrives (still lossless for the vector top k) cuts the guard to 4.78 per note on ENV-F and 2.06 on
  ENV-G, U to 4,448 and 3,195 per 200 notes, with the same tuning recall; it changes nothing on ENV-C, whose library
  notes were decided against the whole library. D19 stores the cosine once; refreshing it is a design question for T51.

## 7. Step 8: throughput forecast and the levers

`W = max(L, R/c) + T_fill + T_tail`, `L = 2·U·t_pair + n_calls·t_call + T_sql`, per 200 notes, with U and n_calls
from B4 (arrival, D19 as written) and every other term at its start value: t_pair 30 ms MLX / 56 ms GGUF (B1
pending), t_call 7 ms (after D38), T_sql 50 ms per note (T49's bar), R/c from the spec's start table at c = 2,
T_fill = the first source's own decide work, T_tail = t_type 2.2 s + t_disc. Today's F2 retrieval SQL takes 725 ms
per note on ENV-C (p95 1.19 s) and 21 ms on ENV-G, so TP-C200 depends on T49.

The spec's formula adds T_fill even when the local lane binds; dropping it (W = L + T_tail) changes no verdict
(TP-G 4.52, TP-C200 4.82 min at K = 20 on MLX).

- **Lever 1 (rankPairsPerCall and helper batch, B1):** at K = 20 the shapes need t_pair ≤15-20 ms on MLX; Spike A's
  25-27 ms amortized and the product adapter's 33.3 ms make that unlikely, so lever 1 alone does not close it.
- **Levers 2 and 3** act on the remote lane; they matter only for TP-F (R/c 172 s), which becomes remote-bound at
  K = 8 (at K = 10, L = 185 s and R/c = 172 s are close).
- **Lever 4 (D17's recall-lossless limits, with the `rules/knowledge.md` edit):** K = 10 is tuning-lossless in arrival
  mode (section 4) and brings every shape under its bar at the start t_pair; K = 8 leaves margin. Its precision side
  (forbidden tuning root pairs reaching the decider fall from 24 to 21) is for the calibration to judge at M1.
- **Not a listed lever:** a refreshed k-th cosine in the guard (section 4) cuts TP-G to 3.56 and TP-F to 4.76 min at
