# F2 decisions: Topic Tree Engine (2026-10-15)

Binding design decisions for phase F2 (`docs/plan.md` decision 18). The plan holds the F2 task boxes, waves and
exit criteria; this file records how and why the engine is built that way. Values marked *(cal.)* are start
values that calibration (T29a, T29b) confirms or revises on measured data. "Q" marks an owner question: each has
a default, and work proceeds on the default until the owner answers. The profiles (ENV-G, ENV-F, ENV-C) and
the exit criteria E1-E6 are defined under Measurement. Spike B start values: `docs/spikes/spike-b-topic-tree.md`.
On 2026-10-15 the owner authorized wiping the legacy wiki early (T00b): D37-D40, Q13 and gate W replace the
switch, the topic modes, the shadow tree and gate D.

## Early legacy wipe (2026-10-15)

- **Authorization, verbatim (owner, chat, 2026-10-15):** "Autorizo apagar totalmente a wiki antiga."
- **As relayed by the orchestrator** (its reading, not the owner's words): the wiki pages, organization,
  maintenance and curator data are deleted completely, with no fallback.
- **Covers:** on the owner's DEV profile, emptying the 30 legacy tables (prefixes `wiki_`, `organization_`,
  `maintenance_`, `automatic_routine_` and `knowledge_impact_`), deleting the settings keys
  `knowledge.wiki-areas.v1`, `knowledge.automation.policy.v1` and `organization.active`, clearing `pageIds`
  in `obsidian.wiki.scope`, and deleting the `organization` and `maintenance` jobs. An agent runs it with
  no confirmation click, only after a restore-verified backup and a rehearsal (`rules/data.md`,
  `rules/security-privacy.md`).
- **Does not cover:** deleting the verified dump or the cold copy of `database/` (only on the owner's
  request), deleting vault files (they are archived through `.memora-recovery`, `rules/integrations.md`),
  any non-legacy table, or a library reset.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Why / alternative rejected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D1 (Q1) | **Topic identity lives in a new table, `topics`**, and `topics.parent_id` is its only parent pointer. `wiki_pages` stays legacy like every other wiki table: emptied by the legacy wipe at gate W (D37), dropped in F4. Plan 4.1 "Ownership" and "Data" are reworded to say this. | A clean schema: no TOC columns, no `human_managed` default and no `parentId` duplicated in revision JSON, and `wiki_pages` is dropped in F4 anyway. The alternative costs about 2 d. |
| D2 | **No topic modes.** After the detach (T35a, T35b), legacy rows are inert data that nothing writes or displays, and every profile runs the real engine. There is no shadow tree, no `topics.mode.v1` marker and no gate-decision mode; tree status is `active\|retired`. Legacy content means rows in `wiki_pages`, `organization_runs`, `organization_settings_revisions`, `maintenance_runs`, `maintenance_schedules`, `wiki_policy_revisions` or `wiki_investigations`; the trigger-fed `knowledge_impact_*` rows do not count. `legacyContentCounts()` (T04) is read only by the wipe, the `LEGACY_RECREATED` detector (D40) and developer status. The wipe's audit record is the setting `legacy.wipe.v1`; fresh and reset profiles need none. | The legacy wiki is wiped at gate W (D37), so no profile keeps a visible legacy wiki to protect. Calibration never needed shadow mode: it runs on offline replays (T09) and ENV profiles (T18, T22a, T22b), and G1 (D23) keeps golden replays free of topic AI calls. |
| D3 | **Legacy state is never created.** T04 stops creation from nothing: `initializeAutomation` runs only when legacy content exists, and full-knowledge admission records `organizeKnowledge` as skipped when no curator policy exists. T35b unwires the remaining writers (startup and reset initialization, ticks, the legacy job executors, the golden pause) and fences their IPC; T10 removes Ask's writers; D40 refuses legacy inserts in the database; T38r-T40r delete the code. | Without this, every reset re-creates the legacy wiki (library reset runs `initializeKnowledgeDefaults`, whose `initializeAutomation` recreates the 9 area pages and the curator policy), the zero-config e2e test fails, and a wiped profile fills up again. |
| D4 | **The wipe is `LegacyWipeService` (T36w), never a migration**, run by the one-shot DEV knob `MEMORA_LEGACY_WIPE=preview\|<token>`. The knob runs after the knowledge defaults and before the local models, the supervisor, the gateway and the vault reconcile start; `preview` and the wipe both quit afterwards, and with no legacy rows, keys or jobs it is a no-op and startup continues. It is refused when packaged or with another DEV knob set (`MEMORA_GOLDEN_RUN`, `MEMORA_GOLDEN_SOURCE_PAIRS`, `MEMORA_GOLDEN_RESET`, `MEMORA_MATCHING_PILOT`, `MEMORA_PROFILE_RESET`). The steps: counts in one snapshot and a token that hashes them (the 30 tables, the legacy settings keys, the legacy jobs), `pg_dump --snapshot`, verify by restore, then one transaction that locks the 30 tables `ACCESS EXCLUSIVE`, recounts and refuses on any difference, truncates them, deletes the keys and jobs, bumps `obsidian_projection_clock` and writes `legacy.wipe.v1`. Backups go to `.cache/f2/legacy-wipe/backups/`. Packaged builds get no trigger in F2. | A migration runs at every start with no backup, and would wipe any restored backup opened later. A count of one table cannot guard 30; the recount under lock closes the window between the dump and the wipe. |
| D5 | **Migration 0052 (T35a, wave 1b) keeps all data and drops the 22 legacy impact triggers, their 4 functions and the FK `atomic_note_evolution.run_id → organization_runs`** (the column stays). It also adds the `obsidian_sync_status` value `retired` (T34a) and the D40 fence. `invalidate_obsidian_projection` stays. | It removes per-row writes on the ingestion hot path, and the wipe's `TRUNCATE` cannot fail on FKs or late-bound plpgsql. |
| D6 | **The legacy tables are emptied at gate W and dropped in F4**, together with the D40 fence. `schema.ts` keeps them until then. | Plan F4; `rules/data.md`. |
| D7 | **Statistics are derived and exact.** Per topic, the stats are `n`, `ls` (Float64 `bytea`), `cos_sq_sum` and the low-cosine percentiles. After any placement change, the touched path is recomputed bottom-up: a leaf is the sum of its members' current vectors, in content-key order; an internal node is the sum of its children's `ls` plus its direct items. In-memory add/subtract is used only inside one engine run and in replay. A member whose vector is missing or stale (an edit pending re-embedding) keeps its placement and is left out of the stats until its new vector exists. Internal nodes sum children in `topics.id` order, so the recompute is bit-stable. Deleted items leave through D32. | The subtract path would need the old vector, which an edit deletes. A per-placement vector copy costs 4 KiB per item and duplicates canonical data. |
| D8 | **Items and counting.** Notes and root sources are both items (I1). `n`/`ls` count notes. A root source counts only while it has no current placed note. Leaf size (I4) counts direct items of both kinds. **Root vector:** `unit(Σ max(0, v·m)^2 · v)` over the root's current notes, with one refinement pass. The fallback is the stored `source-composite-centroid-v2` vector. With neither, the source waits in Unsorted with `needs_vector`. A root is re-placed only when its vector cosine to the last placed vector drops below `items.sourceReplaceCosine` (0.98, *cal.*). | No double counting; a source with no notes stays reachable; no metadata vector is stored to fall back on. |
| D9 | **The single writer is enforced twice.** (1) A `BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE OF parent_id, management` trigger on `topics` rejects an AI-managed write unless the transaction set `memora.topic_writer = 'engine'`. (2) A static test allows only `topicTreeRepository.ts` to write the **structure tables** (`topic_trees`, `topics`, `topic_stats`, `topic_placements`, `topic_change_sets`, `topic_structure_events`) and its own logs (`topic_gate_decisions`, `topic_daily_metrics`, `topic_calibrations`). Other writers are explicit: `topicOverviewRepository.ts` owns the overview tables; DEV seeders and verify scripts write through `topicTreeRepository` (no raw SQL); the only database-side write is D32's cascade and dirty flag. | "Enforced by test" (plan), plus raw SQL in scripts. |
| D10 | **Placement never waits for AI.** `decidePlacement` returns `auto` (best leaf, margin ≥ θm), `gate` (≤5 candidates plus the deepest admitted common ancestor) or `unadmitted` (an ancestor). For `gate`, the item is applied at the fallback ancestor with `needs_gate` and a deduped `topic-gate` job is enqueued. **The gate rule:** only a **validated dedicated ranker** decides; there is no generative gate. Compute `p = softmax([logOdds…, τ]/T)`; accept the best candidate when p ≥ θ, otherwise "none" (`needs_split_check`). ~~With no validated decide route, `needs_gate` stays set, and `topic-sweep` re-enqueues the item once a route validates.~~ **Superseded by owner decision 19 (plan section 2):** with no validated decider the gated item is the gate's "none" (`decided_by = fallback-none`, a movable new-topic candidate REBALANCE may group), and the sweep sends it to the gate again once a ranker validates; the first run installs the ranker. Start values: margin 0.015, T 1, τ 0, θ 0.5, label weight 0. PLACE runs on the light lane's `compute` worker and the gate on its `model` worker (D33), so a gate call waiting for the AI slot never holds a placement. | Spike B: 30-50% of new-topic notes are ambiguous. A gate call queued behind a multi-minute heavy call would stall PLACE. |
| D11 | **Naming.** Operation `topic_name`, class `write-fast` (task `structured-output` when no class route exists). At most 24 topics per call, parent first, aliases in content-key order. The schema has a `language` field that must equal the content language. Validation: not equal to the parent, unique among siblings (accent- and case-insensitive), no ID-shaped strings. One repair re-asks only the invalid items (the F1a matching precedent, applied inside the topic service; the F3 runtime-wide rule is not implemented here). Then a provisional TF-IDF name from content-language notes only, with stopwords for 5 languages. Otherwise `title = null`, `name_state = 'unnamed'`, and the UI renders the localized "Unnamed topic"; no placeholder is stored. **Re-naming happens only** on create, split or merge, or when membership drift since naming is ≥ `naming.renameDrift` (0.3, *cal.*). Human-named topics are never renamed. | Plan 4.1 Naming; Spike B 23/30 names; stability of names. |
| D12 | **Areas** (the top two levels). An LLM proposes a Clio-style grouping of the level-2 topics into 7-16 Areas (target 10, *cal.*; the plan's earlier "~10-16" is re-based on Spike B's 7-12), with pinned Areas fixed. Code validates it: every topic exactly once, size bounds. An answer that fails is repaired in code first (a topic left out joins the Area with the nearest members, a topic named twice stays in the nearest, an Area left empty is dropped) and validated again; the model is asked once more (same operation, under D34's cap) only when more than `ai.areasRepairMaxMissingShare` (0.1) of the topics were missing or the answer is still invalid, and the change-set summary records the repair (`repaired`, `how`). It is accepted only when the mean silhouette of the level-2 centroids under the proposal is not lower than under the current grouping (tolerance *cal.*) and the E2 shape bars still hold. It applies automatically at bootstrap; afterwards **every Area-level operation** (Area regrouping, and any REBALANCE split, merge, dissolve or push-down that changes the set of level-1 topics) becomes `pending_review` (plan 4.3). The root's fan-out band is the Areas band, not I3, so REBALANCE never pushes Areas down. The mis-parent audit is deterministic and goes through REBALANCE acceptance. | Spike B: level 1 was 1/4 coherent and seed-dependent. |
| D13 (Q2) | **Ask is decoupled from the wiki in wave 1b (T10), before the wipe.** It retrieves from notes and chunks only, with no domain or page scope, no "save answer as page" and no followed questions; those writes answer `consultation.errors.retired`, and Ask's error keys move to `consultation.errors.*`. Until Navigator v2 (T20) the Wiki empty state hosts Ask (D38). A topic-subtree scope arrives in T28. Organization domains are dropped. | They write `wiki_pages` through the organization stack, which is wiped and deleted. Questions arrive in F5. |
| D14 | **`organizeKnowledge` leaves the processing DAG in T35a (wave 1b).** Stored plans, runs and batches are read tolerantly: legacy stage IDs read as "skipped (retired)", and the `integrateWiki`/`organization` fields are dropped when parsed. | Admission would throw once the policy is wiped; history must still parse. |
| D15 | **Push channel.** `topicsChanged {treeId, generation, topicIds≤200\|null, changeSetId}`, coalesced over 250 ms after commit; there is no switch progress channel. It follows the `jobsChanged`/`viewHistoryChanged` precedent; the F4 event bus can absorb it. No new polling. | `rules/frontend.md`, `rules/architecture.md`. |
| D16 | **Overviews get their own tables** (`topic_overviews`, `topic_overview_citations`). They are written when the page is opened and cached per member fingerprint, prompt revision, model and language. | The legacy revision tables carry the trigger family and duplicate `parentId`. |
| D17 | **"decide" means a validated dedicated ranker.** It keeps the `reranking` capability. Generative catalog entries lose the `reranking` tag, and `hasGeneration` stops counting it. The validation is stored per install in `local_models.metadata.decideValidation` (artifact SHA-256, runtime build, smoke result, time), and a revision or artifact change invalidates it. `supportsTask` is unchanged. | It is the smallest change that makes code and rule agree. |
| D18 | **No new AI scheduler in F2.** Route order: operation override (`op:<operation>`), then the class route (`class:<class>`) **only when the request sets `workloadClass` explicitly**, then the task route. `class:decide` is seeded only from a validated ranker, the MLX ranker first when the host runs MLX, else the GGUF one (decision 12); `class:write-fast` from the writer route, under the privacy rule. **Minimal residency, pulled from F3 (Q11):** one MLX helper process holds at most one `generate`, one `embed` and one `score` model; `AiService` keeps one embedding and one ranker adapter resident beside the one generative adapter; rankers are exempt from idle release, like embeddings. Idle unload of the generative model and group sizing stay in F3. Light-lane AI calls use the existing `background` queue class. | A third queue class gains nothing while the heavy lane issues one call at a time. Real lanes are F3. Without residency every gate call between embedding calls reloads both models. |
| D19 | **The ambiguity bar is re-based** (Spike B condition 5). The bars *(cal.)*: (a) auto-placement precision ≥90% on known topics; (b) of new-topic notes, ≤50% gated; (c) gate p95 ≤0.3 s per gated note (target 0.2 s). Gate precision on new topics is reported against the margin-only baseline. | The 30% bar measured gate capacity, not quality. |
| D20 (Q3) | **Owner rating rule:** a topic counts only if rated **both** coherent and well named, and the bar is ≥24/30 such topics. The two marginals are reported. | It is the literal reading of the exit text. It gates the close of F2 (gate C), not the wipe. |
| D21 | **Developer comparison panel:** a generated HTML report (`npm run topics:report`). It reads `.f32` replays and in-app `topics:export` JSON, never a live owner database. | No product copy, IPC or i18n for developer tooling. |
| D22 | **Determinism by content key.** Clustering rows, prompt aliases, gate candidate order and stats summation follow note content hash, then ID. | `rules/ai.md`: prompts are a function of content. Cassette keys and the rebuilt tree must be stable across replays with fresh UUIDs. |
| D23 | **G1: `MEMORA_GOLDEN_TOPICS=deterministic\|full`, read only when `MEMORA_GOLDEN_RUN` is set.** Every non-golden run (DEV, packaged, E6) behaves as `full`. In a golden run the default `deterministic` places and rebalances but makes no topic AI call (no gate, naming, Areas, labels or overview). F2 measurements use `full` in `record` mode on cassette copies. **Topic barrier:** in `full` golden runs, ENV-C seeding and the E6 harness, topic jobs are held while a source ingests and the light lane is drained at each source boundary (or once at the end with `MEMORA_GOLDEN_TOPICS_ARRIVAL=batch`), so the items present at every build and naming call are a function of the manifest order. `per-source` is the default arrival in `full` runs. | Existing replays (F1b/F3) keep 0 misses and their timing; recorded `topic.*` calls replay at 0 misses. |
| D24 | **`objects.locate`:** `primaryPath` = the topic ancestors (root to primary topic) followed by the existing source chain; `alsoIn` = secondary placements (≤2); `citedBy` = overviews that cite the object; the `legacy` flag goes. | The F1b contract comment announced this shape (`object-address.ts`). |
| D25 (Q9) | **No MLX embedding adapter in F2.** The engine uses stored note vectors in the active route's space. T01's protocol supports `embed`, so F3 can add the adapter. | A new space forces a library re-embed; MLX and GGUF vectors agree at cosine 0.9996. |
| D26 (Q8) | **Embedding space.** A pure `embeddingSpaceKey(selection, adapterIdentity)` is extracted from `ai-service.ts`. `activeEmbeddingSpace()` computes the route's space **without a call** and confirms it against the newest stored note vector. When the tree's space differs from the route's, the status is `space_changed`: PLACE of new-space items waits, and current notes are re-embedded through the existing `note-embedding` jobs (automatically for local routes; through an explicit action for remote routes). At ≥95% coverage *(cal.)* the tree rebuilds in the new space and is mapped by Hungarian matching. Spaces are never compared. | `rules/data.md` vectors rule; plan 4.1. |
| D27 | **Unsorted.** In the navigator, "Sem lugar / Unsorted" is the direct items of the root. An item waiting at an internal ancestor with `needs_gate` or `needs_split_check` is shown in that topic's item list with an "unsorted" marker. The **Unsorted share KPI** counts both groups; roots waiting with `needs_vector` (import-only sources, catalog containers without text) are reachable but reported apart, not in the share. Ordinary direct items of an internal topic (the mockup's "2 + 6 diretos") are not unsorted. | Mockups `Topic.dc.html` ("Sem lugar 3") and `Map.dc.html` ("1,9%", "2 + 6 diretos"). |
| D28 (Q5) | **Pin.** Pinning a topic is one user action with two effects in one change-set: a `view_history` pin (the navigator's "Fixados / Pinned") and `topics.management = 'pinned'`, which protects the topic. Undoing a pin or unpin change-set restores both effects in one transaction. | One canonical protection flag; "pinned nodes never move automatically" (plan 4.1). |

| D32 | **Deleted items leave by cascade.** `topic_placements.note_id` and `source_item_id` are `ON DELETE CASCADE`. A statement-level `AFTER DELETE ...
